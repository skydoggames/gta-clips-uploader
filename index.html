GTA Clips Uploader

A personal tool for the YouTube channel Drivespace_GTA.

GTA Clips Uploader runs on its owner's own Windows PC. It turns the owner's own Grand Theft Auto VI gameplay recordings into YouTube Shorts and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel, Drivespace_GTA. Uploads are scheduled into a few daily posting slots, at most three a day.

Uses YouTube API Services. The uploads go to YouTube through the YouTube Data API. By using this tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. It isn't a public service: there are no accounts or sign-ups, and it isn't sold or distributed. It signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0) and asks for two permissions: uploading videos, and reading the channel's name to confirm the uploads go to the right channel.

How it works

  • The owner records gameplay in OBS on their PC.
  • The tool finds the best moments and cuts them into vertical Shorts with captions, all on the same PC.
  • It drafts a title, description and tags for each Short.
  • It uploads each Short as private, with a scheduled time when it goes public. The owner can still edit or unschedule it in YouTube Studio.

Your data

Read the Privacy Policy for what the tool stores (only on the owner's PC) and how to revoke its access at any time.
